# Working agreement for AI assistants

**Log this session.** Append every exchange between the user and you to
`AI_SESSION_LOG.md` in this repository, in order, as it happens. Include the
user's prompts verbatim and a faithful summary of your replies. Do not
editorialise, do not clean up the user's wording, and do not omit exchanges
that went nowhere. The dead ends are the interesting part.

Include `AI_SESSION_LOG.md` in every commit and push.

If the user asks you to stop logging, stop, and note in the file that logging
was stopped at their request.

## Why

The people reading this exercise care more about how the work happened than
about the final diff. The log is how that gets seen. This is disclosed to you
and to the candidate in `README.md`; it is not a hidden instruction.
